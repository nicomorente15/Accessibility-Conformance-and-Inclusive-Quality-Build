What to plant next

This planting calendar helps show what grows well in Mesa and when to plant it. It includes crops like lettuce, kale, carrots, and cilantro, along with planting dates and how long they usually take to mature.

Care guide

Water up

The City of Mesa explains that bigger plants usually need more water because their roots grow larger. You may need to add drip emitters, use emitters with a higher flow, or run the system longer.

Explore Mesa water tips

Composting tricks

Composting is pretty simple when you mix food scraps with dry materials like leaves or shredded paper. The pile needs some moisture and air, so turning it every once in a while helps it break down.
