A community garden in Mesa, AZ

Growing side by side in Mesa

Rooted Together is a 36-plot garden at 104 E Glade Ave in Mesa, where neighbors grow vegetables, herbs, and flowers through the seasons.

Find your placeLearn our story
Open garden hours
Saturday mornings, 8–11am

Why this place matters

Gardeners trade tips, share extra harvests, and keep learning together.

Folks here swap advice about shade cloth, tomato starts, and the best time to water. When a bed produces more than one household can use, the extra often goes home with someone else down the row.
